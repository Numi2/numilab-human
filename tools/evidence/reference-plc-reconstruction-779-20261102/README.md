This package preserves the versioned 779 reference-PLC producer and its tiny source-only controls. The fixture covers exact paired Skin/Fat shared-interface orientation and readback, an inverted interior cell repaired with an exact readback, and rejection of a self-intersecting shell.

## Recorded controls

The pinned source-control manifest and compact reports identify the fixture inputs, output hashes, independent readback, command lines, and dependency pins. The tiny NPZ fixture/input/output payloads and TetGen executable are not copied; their exact upstream paths and hashes remain in the source-control manifest. The package includes the reports and operation journal only.

## Dependencies and limits

The producer's topology, exact-volume, intersection, quality-readback, partial-solver, source, and TetGen dependencies are referenced by path and SHA-256 in `controls/source-control-reference-plc-779.json`; none are copied here. The full-mesh reconstruction remains `NOT_RUN_SOURCE_I_FACE_GATE_PENDING`; mass mapping is `NOT_RUN`; native steps are zero; physical admission is false. This is not a physical or human-qualification result.
