This bundle preserves the 1218 source fit, rejected held-out check, local reduction attempts 001–006, the root001 audit setup failure, the successful root003 nine-pose exact audit, and the composed source candidate. Large `.nhskin`, `.nhanatomy`, `.nhtissue`, `.mrvpack`, solver arrays, and anatomy-receipt assets remain at their original paths and are pinned in `external-artifacts.json`.

## Result boundary

The initial three-pose CPU fit is an inferred engineering clearance candidate, not measured tissue deformation. It used accepted states 0, 9983, and 20000, fixed 32 bed-support vertices and 15 thorax anchors, and retained 86 skin bindings and source topology. Its maximum displacement from the starting skin was 20.0604 mm across 4,899 active source vertices. The six-pose held-out scan was an offline forward prediction on captured body poses, not a candidate native capture. It found zero skin-to-target crossings and zero degenerates, but exact skin self-intersections remained at steps 4991/5375/5759: 14, 10, and 6 pairs respectively. Steps 6111/6495/7743 had zero self pairs. That fit candidate was rejected as a complete clearance candidate.

The stable64 refinement was checked separately on six held-out states. The original strict single-connected-shell check is retained as a rejection: stable64 has three closed oriented components, so it is not one connected component. A separate explicit nested-enclosure supplement (report SHA `f51e63e91867f63a5a65b67af501f171b325804bc97033b26a9b412edd307024`, also pinned in `external-artifacts.json`) then checked the full 6,690-face row under `allow_nested_enclosure=True`. At all six poses, the 6,666-face component contains each of the two 12-face components, the small components are disjoint from each other, cross-component intersections are zero, and self-intersections and degenerates are zero. That supplement marks it eligible as an external skin enclosure under the nested-envelope rule; it does not make stable64 a single connected shell or qualify native capture, skin-to-target clearance, continuous time, or physics.

Local reductions 001–006 are retained with their separate outcomes. Attempt 001's two uniform patch scalars were infeasible. Attempt 002 had a linear-feasible fallback but its quadratic solve failed, so it was not selected. Attempt 003 hit the QP iteration limit and emitted no proposal. Attempt 004 solved the bounded per-vertex reduction problem; the maximum correction relative to the preceding fit candidate was 0.90586 mm. The QP support comprised 106 optimized vertices and 291 incident face rows (`affected_face_count` in the feasibility report), subject to 24,777 linear constraints. A separate post-solve source-shape comparison found 106 changed vertex positions and 288 face rows with changed geometry; its `changed_face_count` has a different definition from the QP support count. Attempts 005 and 006 replayed that same proposal byte-for-byte while expanding dependency pin coverage (542 and 547 tracked files, respectively); their output proposal SHA is identical. In attempt 006, the legacy report key `loaded_module_files_pinned=547` counts tracked files, not imported Python modules. These remain source proposals, not anatomical measurements.

The root001 nine-pose audit attempt failed before geometry scanning because it required equal packed vertex-map hashes across cohorts; the refined NHTISS changes global influence-buffer offsets by +3 although the source mapping fields match. Root003 instead evaluates each cohort with its own receipt-bound map, without relaxing the geometric predicate. It completed the full exact scan for 9 accepted states: 0/859 target intersections, 0 skin self-intersections, 0 degenerate skin faces, 0 invalid target triangles, and 0 inside vertices for both closed-envelope checks at every pose. Input hashes were unchanged. The poses were 0, 9983, 20000 from native1217 and 4991, 5375, 5759, 6111, 6495, 7743 from retained1191 captures. This is an offline source-proposal forward audit, not a native replay or continuous-time guarantee. The absolute minimum signed bed gap was -0.000106431544 m; the captured-baseline minimum was -0.000106431544 m. The report's gate checks non-worsening and orientation; the negative absolute gap remains visible here. Minimum source/mapped triangle areas were 8.05482525e-09 m² and 8.16800405e-09 m²; minimum source-to-pose normal alignment was 0.315381.

The composition report is `source_candidate_composed_native_pending`; it records `candidate_admitted: false` and `native_run_performed: false`. It reports unchanged NHA payload and functional bindings, unchanged refined NHTISS, 86 bindings, indices/face order, and flat-bed support, with normals recomputed by the existing owner. Those are composition-stage checks. A separate root-owned 310-second native evaluation was active during this bundle staging; its outcome is not included here and must be assessed from its own retained run evidence.
