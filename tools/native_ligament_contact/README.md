# Native superior glenohumeral ligament contact

The full-volume resting scene on SSH Numi previously stopped at the bilateral superior glenohumeral ligament rows. The entheses were on their registered bones. The straight line between them entered bone. This implementation retains both entheses and the existing tension law and applies the law to the exterior material path. All other ligament rows retain their existing force owner.

The reference path has a fixed scapular prefix, a material pivot and a sliding suffix. Five left and three right edge coordinates are sufficient at this entrance. Metal minimizes the length of the suffix at the current relative rigid pose and checks the edge coordinates, stationarity, edge and pivot normal cones and insertion orientation. A failed check rejects the physical transaction. The endpoint directions give the generalized forces and torques through the existing body Jacobian. The force and audit kernels share the same evaluator. The active branch is specific to the registered entrance. Different contact branches require a further physical route solve.

Run `bash tools/native_ligament_contact/verify.sh /path/to/new/build` on SSH Numi with the retained Human and Matter owner checkouts, native inputs, Apple Metal compiler and preparation environment. It verifies the input hashes, builds the matched executable, adapter and metallib, tests the production kernels and launches the integrated full-volume scene. The default admission test requests one accepted step. Geometry, physiological assets, source identities and the recorded mass allocation stay with their existing owners. The authoring code verifies the respiratory banks, rigid frames and intercostal attachments before binding the unchanged thorax to the new skeleton hash. It also verifies the cardiac sidecars referenced by that binding.

On an Apple M6 running macOS 27.0 (26A425), both production Metal kernels matched the independent double-precision solver across 84 poses. There were 54 admitted cases and 30 expected branch rejections. The maximum length error was 0.050 µm, the maximum generalized force error was 0.00082 N and the maximum generalized torque error was 0.00011 N·m. Four straight-ligament regression cases also passed. These fixtures load the ligament with 1% prestrain to exercise the wrench calculation. The integrated scene retains its original reference prestrain.

The native entrance accepted all 34 ligament rows and included all 26 fascia regions, 318,226 deformable nodes, the verified skin/fat mass transfer and the variable-pleural-pressure owner. The first accepted step advanced 15.625 µs in 24.13 s of body runtime, or 6.48 × 10⁻⁷ real time. Process launch to exit took 167.40 s. The bed carried 1.57 N on the FEM skin. The 97.13 kg assembly was still in release. Supported rest requires sustained reaction close to its weight of 953 N. The entrance test cannot establish respiratory cycling, motion clearance or sustained throughput.
