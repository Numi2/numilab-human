The reference assembly includes BodyParts3D FJ2810, copyright The Database Center for Life Science, [CC BY 4.0](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html). It is a mixed-source reference assembly, not measurements of one person. The new local surfaces are inferred geometric reconstructions from the pinned skin and registered bone envelope, with constructive provenance and explicit 0.8 mm kernel radius. This radius is a geometry construction parameter, not a validated skin/fat thickness.

Offline Boolean authoring uses [Manifold 3.5.1](https://github.com/elalish/manifold), Apache-2.0. It is not a replacement runtime or per-step physics implementation. The native Matter runtime remains authoritative.
