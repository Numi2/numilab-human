# Local skin/fat retet routing and source330 input preparation

The authoring tools consume an explicit selected-cell retet packet. They preserve outside-used coordinates, outside cells, semantic groups and the exact selected/outside cut triangle set. Selected-only old nodes may retire. A protected canonical row may name an explicit retet descendant; this identity routing does not establish anatomical target validity. Every such target must later pass the native attachment, source-lineage, material, contact and mass owners.

The assembler emits existing geometry/node sidecars and base-to-output identity maps. The source330 packer validates independent historical source registration and operational support in a positive incident candidate cell. It emits the existing native node/element/label and 54-column node-map formats, and it never fabricates one-hot original-node identity for a derived vertex. Source history carries its authored residual and registration limit. Operational support retains the 0.4 mm bound. Native physical and mass admission remain separate and mandatory.

Public entry points are tools/splice_selected_tissue_retet.py and tools/prepare_spliced_child_inputs_source330.py. Their shared checks live in tools/human_skin_fat_retet_mapping.py. Both CLIs expose their required packet arguments with --help. No TetGen, runtime or mass allocation is invoked by the assembler.

Twelve focused tests pass on SSH Numi, including identity, one-to-four cell refinement, exact outside/cut preservation, selected-only retirement, retargeted protected-node CLI packaging, semantic rejection and stale/nonincident mapping rejection. The executable source330 test deliberately uses a large synthetic historical residual to prove domain separation; it is not an approved Human registration distance or a physical tolerance change.

The published helper contains one active assembler definition. Two superseded prototype definitions and their dead face-merge helper were removed before retesting the exact publication files. The source-only 671 control and its receipt remain retained separately.

No physical Human mesh candidate, full-body zero-crossing boundary, mass-transfer result, Runtime or motion result is established by these tests.
