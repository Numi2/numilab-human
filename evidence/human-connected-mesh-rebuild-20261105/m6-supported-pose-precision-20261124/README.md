# Registered skin precision and supported-pose reconstruction on Apple M6

The full volumetric scene starts in release. The two-step capture carried 11.007 N of 952.862 N of normal weight through 16 skin nodes. Its body and physiology clocks advanced together for 15.625 µs. The body took 70.839 s (2.206 × 10⁻⁷ real time), and the cold launch including capture took 215.314 s (7.257 × 10⁻⁸ real time). Variable pleural pressure and the original contact, material, ligament and equilibrium checks were enabled.

## Position storage

The loaded-pose reader previously required every current world coordinate to equal its single Float32 conversion. Matter already stores its material reference and the high and low words of displacement. The reader and the bonded material author now use that representation. A sub-Float32 position is admitted only when the registered displacement mode is active and the compiler can reconstruct the input from its two displacement words and its stored reference. The file digest, attachment identities, cortical source binding, bed and shape checks still apply.

A 0.005-radian rigid rotation of the 1,457,312 registered tetrahedra preserves their shape in exact arithmetic. Storing the rotated vertices in one absolute Float32 word produced 339 inversions and 1,691 total geometry failures. The existing native reference-plus-two-word storage produced zero of either. The production Metal orientation predicate reproduced the storage-dependent result in all 980 test cases on M6. This regression measures the precision of initialization.

The full native run used two conservatively remapped interior sample sites and their source barycentric coordinates. The updated whole authoring fit selected the same two sites. The separate matched reconstruction agreed within 1.4 × 10⁻¹⁷ m at the sites. Reference positions, masses, and the complete set of skin/fat boundary positions were preserved. Both native steps passed. At the endpoint, four skin cells and 34 fat cells missed the shape threshold, zero cells inverted, and the four registered tissue surfaces had zero intersecting pairs. The 185-bone solid audit still found 5.97643 cm³ of summed pairwise overlap. A three-step request rejected its third step with fresh equilibrium residual 0.0318247. The earlier two-word runtime already represented accepted displacements accurately. Initializing the two interior sites with higher precision did not resolve the third-step rejection.

## Starting from supported anatomy

The existing reduced 16-second scene provides measured support sites and an articulated pose. At 7.998 s its accepted skeleton and the following contact impulses have matching time indices. The 952.846 N sample mapped to the current skin by registered body. Sites accounting for 818.122 N intersected the current exterior within 20 mm of the bed. Both heel sites were about 50 mm above the bed. These are geometric correspondences across two model configurations.

All 122 scalar joint coordinates were recovered from the 157 accepted body poses. Source forward kinematics reproduced every body position within 0.164 µm. The current payload changes two clavicular joint frames. Fitting six independent shoulder coordinates to the supported body with the current equality map reduced whole-skeleton RMS position error from 17.78 to 11.45 mm. Maximum angular discrepancy rose from 0.131 to 0.188 radians. The candidate satisfies the authored scalar joint limits. Anatomical and dynamic qualification of the new pose remains open.

Direct transfer through the known material parents left 12,518 inverted cells at the full candidate pose. A joint solve for a continuous displacement over the bonded mesh reduced that count to 2,244 and still placed some exterior nodes below the bed. Both counts use the historical absolute Float32 emission. Neither candidate was installed. A local posterior nonlinear preparation independently equilibrated 5% of body weight on the plane with zero inversions, but 24 cells failed the loader's shape or volume-ratio gates. At 12.5% load its contact tangent lost positive definiteness. The local preparation freezes its surrounding boundary and cannot establish whole-body support.

Use the supported native skeleton and measured bed reactions as initial estimates for a joint, tissue and bed solve. The running simulation must generate its own reactions. The current full volumetric scene still requires a viable material configuration in that pose, a supported mechanical equilibrium, and sustained coupled verification. Neither the reduced scene nor this microsecond capture satisfies the integrated 16-second performance and crossing targets.

Exact run commands, binaries, compact traces, rejected candidates, registered identities, mesh checks and the executable tests are retained in the independently downloaded and SHA-256-verified release listed in recovery.json. The four closed preview base packs with missing APFS compression flags were restored and verified against the accepted capture digest.

## Supported geometry with compensated coordinates

Both supported-pose proposals were re-evaluated with the exact reference-plus-high-and-low displacement accepted by the native loader. The material-parent transfer left 12,250 inverted tetrahedra and 32,544 nonpassing cells at the full pose. The continuous whole-volume fit left 1,952 inversions and 7,171 nonpassing cells. The latter includes 0.013275 kg of registered material. Of the nonpassing tetrahedra, 4,570 have all four vertices on registered skin/fat surfaces. Its lowest exterior position is 10.72 mm below the fixed bed. The transferred pose remains a rejected initialization. The results identify the regional material owners and distinguish geometric defects from absolute-coordinate rounding.
