## Why the candidate cannot enter the complete scene

The captured physical-floor reference at initialization contains the detailed external skin (semantic 51007, stable 1), FEM inner skin (0xFFFFFD11) and deep fat boundary (0xFFFFFD12). Against the external skin, the right and left clavicles introduced 39 and 70 triangle pairs and the right and left scapulae introduced 527 and 462. The parent had zero for these four outer-skin interfaces. Inner-skin pairs went from zero to 1,178; deep-fat pairs went from four to 1,299. The tissue comparison uses frozen native tissue from the separately identified physical-floor configuration. Counts include paired surface crossings, regardless of the intended adjacency of structures. The rigid-fit candidate cannot be used as a skin correction.

The source contains named superior glenohumeral and coracohumeral passive routes on both sides. Each has two sites, a side site, a 20 mm humeral sphere, a positive reference length and zero donor stiffness. The force owner must supply a justified law separately. Transferring the donor route geometry onto the nearest material triangles moves individual endpoints by 0.03–1.55 mm. Curved segments were sampled with the maximum chord sag reported per row. A bounded footprint search tested 44,058 pairs of sampled material points within 6 mm of the named source sites. None met both outward tangent conditions across the sentinel poses. This rules out the tested assignments and wrap, while leaving other surface-contact routes open.

The original common BodyParts3D atlas also yielded no clear straight candidate among the tested bony footprints for the four capsular rows.
