SSH Numi, Apple M6, native Metal. The test uses the registered 7,233-node,
28,632-tetrahedron parent model. The comparison copies the launch from the
eight-step replay and keeps its material, solver, cardiac, respiratory,
ligament and bed contact configuration. None of the force laws, solver gates
or step sizes change. This is an opt-in calibration candidate.

The registration audit uses the compiled Float32 node locations and all 86
admitted collision banks from the running native owner. It tests the 2,411
outer and 2,411 deep-fat nodes, using bank bounding boxes and three ray
parity checks, with deterministic nearest surface witnesses for interior
points. This is an initial point-inside-bank audit. It cannot rule out
surface intersections between vertices or characterize clearance in motion.

The baseline has 128 inside point-bank pairs. The strongest is outer row
337 against body 59, bone 95, at 20.86 N estimated from the registered
initial contact law. This is the same patch as the fat element that
prevented acceptance of step nine in the previous replay.

refined cells had a vertex outside their own parent and 22 parents failed

follow-up fit constrains changes on original edges and their 79 midpoint
descendants for all three layer roles, keeping the same explicit parent
partition. The preflight measured zero child containment errors or
nonpositive cells and a maximum relative parent coverage error of
1.31e-12. The skin and fat volume constraints apply independently in
each fitted stencil. Individual nodal masses are regenerated by the
existing compiler. The compiled skin/fat sum changed by less than
0.08 mg and the body trace reports zero mass residual.

(4.21e-6× including preparation and export). The terminal capture
