# Contact initialization follow-up

A separate exploratory follow-up uses the existing temporary penalty
initializer and the existing force-to-positive-gap mapping. The native IPC
energy and final residual remain the authority, and the temporary penalty
has no part in accepted trial forces. Only independent re-equilibration at
a load fraction qualifies that fraction. The follow-up tests whether
engagement at the 10 µm activation band is the limiting numerical problem.
