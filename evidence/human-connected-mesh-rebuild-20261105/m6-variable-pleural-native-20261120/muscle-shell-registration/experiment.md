# Pose-registered muscle clearance

The accepted shared-tissue root 16 capture is the training reference. The
previously qualified regional variable-pressure root 8000 is a transfer screen
with the same captured muscle identities and different skin geometry and pose.
The comparison is limited to these two frozen native geometries. It cannot
qualify the continuous shared-tissue motion.

The current asset is selected through the exact native launch receipt.
Determine whether rotational blend distortion drives some of the forearm
crossings by evaluating dual-quaternion transport for registered, eligible
muscles. Require native linear-blend replay error below 0.12 mm in each pose,
uniform per-bank scale, positive finite blended rotations, and source face
identity. Screen fixed fractions 0.25, 0.5 and 1.0. Geometry changes require
fewer target intersections in both poses, no newly intersecting skin region
outside the original witness neighborhoods, preservation of triangle
orientation and no added skin, bone or neighboring muscle crossings in the
subsequent exact gate. A failure of rigid transport on the same poses
redirects work to a source clearance fit constrained by anatomy and exact
accepted geometry. Never use a successful experiment as authority to change a
force, mass or computational contact law.

Source-fit screening keeps the original skin and pose fixed and applies
smooth local displacements to source muscle vertices. Constraints come from
the current intersections and their local signed skin depth, and are refreshed
from exact candidate intersections. Pinned attachment collars and coincident
source seams retain identity. Require at most 0.5% surface volume-proxy and
triangle orientation ratio of at least 0.60 in rounded source and posed
geometry. Evaluate both poses and require the same whole-operand crossing
rules before native admission. This screening can reject models and produce
source candidates. Native admission also requires neighbor and self audits
and reconstruction using the real asset owner.
