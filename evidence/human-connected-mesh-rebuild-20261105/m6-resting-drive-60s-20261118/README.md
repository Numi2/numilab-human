# Matched 60-second native respiratory-drive trial

Both native runs use the exact published 360d278c scene, 30,000 consecutive accepted 2 ms steps and sampled viewer presentation on Apple M6. The same airflow, circulation, gas and Brain owners run in both arms. The test changed drive to 0.60 from 8 to 20 s. The 500 prior samples are identical. Each arm recorded 11 completed breaths and 70 complete cardiac filling/ejection cycles. Accepted viewer-inclusive rates were 0.105395× and 0.105375×. Each process used about 580 s cold. No root assistance was applied. Both had late support within 0.001 N of modeled body weight and 939 passing sampled viewer geometry checks. The largest sampled blood volume error was 0.0182 mL.

The drive reduction cut inspired volume by 26.8%. The later response-window average arterial CO₂ was 0.969 mmHg higher and arterial O₂ was 2.459 mmHg lower. In the final window these differences fell to +0.012 and −0.294 mmHg, passing both preregistered gas recovery checks. [Published cardiopulmonary modeling and gas response](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/full/10.1152/ajpheart.00923.2014)

The preregistered fixed-window ventilation recovery gate failed. Treatment inspired volume was 1.399× of control from 52 to 60 s. The completed-breath markers occur at the next inspiration onset. Both arms logged two such markers in the window, with different timing. Treatment logged them at 52.450 and 57.890 s, and control at 54.050 and 59.558 s. At 60 s the control was inhaling, while treatment was exhaling. An exploratory event-aligned analysis after 40 s found 526.2 versus 523.1 mL per completed breath and 5.433 versus 5.516 s between inspirations. These exploratory metrics leave the original threshold result intact. The qualification plan, unmodified gate result, exact launch hashes and compact traces are included alongside this file.

Aortic ejection is identical in every matched sample. This version uses a fixed circulatory extramural pressure during the drive intervention. It supports the measured ventilation-to-gas mechanism. Dynamic vascular pressure coupling and 330 s qualification require separate evidence.
