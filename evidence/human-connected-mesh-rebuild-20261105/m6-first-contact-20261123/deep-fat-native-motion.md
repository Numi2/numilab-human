The whole-body anatomical audit covered 878 banks. It recorded 10,132 skin-to-tissue triangle pairs, zero skin-to-bone pairs, zero skin self-intersections and zero layer crossings at the new endpoint. The bed carried 0.0503% of body weight in the first-contact release. The cold rate was 9.99e−8×, and the body timing window measured 1.01e−6×. These observations establish neither supported rest nor complete respiratory and cardiac cycles.

A matched two-root control at the same final time showed a large initial sensitivity of the FEM-to-skeleton load. The first root force changed from approximately (2.87, 16.76, −15.72) N to (10.91, 61.94, −66.52) N when the step was halved. On the next half step it reversed to (−10.48, −58.63, 68.84) N. The registered fixed-anchor reaction history also rose from 23.78 to 91.27 N in mean magnitude. This justifies testing initialization and the interface impulse balance before another contour fit.
