# Posterior deltoid force registration in the supported Human

Both posterior deltoid origins previously missed the installed scapular cortex by about 48.8 mm. Each origin was 0.055 mm from its own MyoSim source scapula. This mixed-source reference adult needs explicit registration from the muscle skeleton onto the installed bone surface. A postprocessing skin edit cannot repair that force path.

I placed each origin on an actual cortical triangle adjacent to the atlas spinal deltoid and compiled four traction nodes on each bone through the existing tendon owner. The points are inferred within a broad anatomical attachment area. They are 1.00–1.10 mm from the registered muscle surface. The modifications preserve the wrapped path length, shoulder leverage and muscle and tendon parameters. The proximal wrapping sphere and its side selector move about the fixed scapular intermediate site. The physical origin and all eight traction nodes were enclosed at release and in the sampled native motion. The two intermediate sites remain exterior.

An alternative that enclosed the intermediate sites shortened a roughly 153 mm path to 97 mm. I rejected it. The emitted Float32 programs were checked on six reference or captured poses and 64 seeded shoulder perturbations per side. Maximum length difference was 1.52e-10 m and maximum signed moment-arm vector difference was 4.81e-13 m for this reference evaluator. Native trajectories and geometry were checked separately. ## Reproduce the registered inputs

The independently recovered archive contains the paired typed native muscle/tendon assets in `qualified-input`, the exact dense launch, its terminal accepted geometry and the source correspondence. The compact recovery includes all captured poses and the original executable invocation. [Recovered registration and dense inputs](shoulder-registration-recovery.json) · [Recovered compact run](shoulder-supported-8000-recovery.json)

With a matching Human launch and the recovered registration directory, run the following from the repository root. The binding command uses Python 3.11 or later. It verifies each active muscle, tendon and bone identity, writes a new launch and leaves all solver and physiology settings intact.

```sh
tools=tools/native_ligament_contact/registered_bed_work/first_contact/material_response
python3 "$tools/bind_registered_shoulder.py" "$parent_launch" "$registered_inputs" "$new_launch"
```

On the prepared SSH Numi workspace the existing owner runner can also schedule a fresh replay with a new output directory. The compact and dense controls have different physical timesteps. The input bindings, full argv, environment and asset pins generated by this runner were compared with the two completed native executions. [Launcher regression](shoulder-launcher-parity.json)

```sh
export PYTHONPATH="$tools:tools/native_ligament_contact/registered_digit_material:${PYTHONPATH:-}"
python3 "$tools/run_shoulder_scene.py" "$parent_run" "$registered_inputs" "$new_run" "$step_count"
```
