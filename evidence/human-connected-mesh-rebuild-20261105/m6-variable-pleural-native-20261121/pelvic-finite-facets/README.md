# Finite cortical interface contact in supported native motion

Both muscles now solve and transmit full constitutive forces in the native articulated transaction. Their cortical contact includes 16,414 source-registered cut triangles on the pelvis, sacrum and femora. Each material triangle is projected onto its finite cortical parent. The GPU clips the overlap, integrates the P1 material shape and signed gap over that area, and applies the nonnegative resultant at the corresponding material point. A deepest-point row and the bilateral closed-solid queries retain separate clearance checks. An empty projected area supplies no face pressure. The existing bulk forces, skeletal attachments, bed and physiological owners participate in the same accepted transaction.

This implementation uses finite projected areas and a constant pressure mode on each clipped patch. The [Puso and Laursen segment-to-segment formulation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0045782503005802) provides background on segment-based contact. The Numi implementation uses its own working-set solve and a fresh physical residual at each accepted endpoint.

The original sacral vertex-only force assignment failed the first 2 ms step with variable pleural pressure. Finite faces passed eight consecutive steps and then reached 23 accepted steps in the requested 16 s trial. At 46 ms, independently integrating material energy gave free nodal residuals of 4.40 µN right and 12.67 µN left. At this pose 124 face-average rows, 14 deepest-point rows and four vertex rows carried nonzero pressure. Compared at those same body poses, the closed-solid overlap decreased from 0.00323538 mL before the elastic correction to 0.000002762 mL after it. Exact native Float32 coordinates still have 2,008 transverse triangle pairs across the six regional interfaces.

On the next pre-dynamics candidate the left residual reached 0.01175 N against a 0.05874 N contact-force scale. The 1% physical gate rejected the step. Brain and respiration remained at 46 ms. The measured 0.046 s in 17.662 s is 0.00260× including launch, below the end-to-end target. The supported scene and these local muscles do not establish whole-body tissue settling, full deep-fat contact, 16 seconds or zero crossings.

The exact production Metal clip kernel passed six independent projected-polygon comparisons covering overlap, sliding, disjoint and edge-only contact, and relative bone motion. The largest measured gap error was 1.14e-11 m in those fixtures. Two stand-alone audits also checked the full constitutive forces and closed-solid intersections at the published endpoints. Source registration, binaries, configuration, launch commands, accepted captures and failed-step evidence are retained.
