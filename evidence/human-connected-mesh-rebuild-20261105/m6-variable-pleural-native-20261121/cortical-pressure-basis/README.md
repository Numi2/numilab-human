At 286 ms the bilateral gluteal solver carried 89 positive pressure rows on the right and 253 on the left. Counting complete force columns on every tissue node and each bone's force and moment, the left active set had 201 distinct columns. The right had 89. Relative singular-value cutoffs of 1e−6 and 1e−10 in the row-scaled matrix yielded left ranks of 136 and 149. These are measurements of the accepted active set.

This variant combines positive loads only when anatomical owners, the four P1 force weights and parents, surface normal, lever arm and both current gap measurements agree. It compares the node support without treating zero-weight nodes as force carriers. Each original surface pair remains eligible for the next geometry test. A distinct-owner, gap, normal, moment arm or even one-ULP weight difference leaves the rows separate. The included GPU fixtures exercise these cases.
