# Finite contact features through 120 ms

Each pressure row now follows the same material vertex, cortical vertex, or pairing of two physical edges as the surfaces slide. The area-centroid pressure remains available. The source of each contact force and the opposite cortical reaction remain the original registered structures. Previously a single minimum-gap row could change its point of application when the ordering of two gap values reversed. The new Metal implementation clips the same two finite triangles and keeps a separate stable identifier for each possible intersection feature. Compiling with the preceding two-row assets remains supported. At step eight that configuration reproduced the accepted material state and native surface bytes exactly.

The Metal tests compared all emitted feature IDs, positions and gaps with an independent intersection of half-planes. They covered sliding, moving cortex, coincidence along an edge, a polygon enclosing the cortical triangle, and reversal of the direction of the separation slope. The test for slope reversal confirmed the pressure rows stayed at the same material coordinates.

On SSH Numi the coupled native scene accepted 60 consecutive 2 ms steps with variable pleural pressure. A separate run completed the same 120 ms in 16.375 wall seconds, or 0.00733× including startup. It reproduced the exact displacements and emitted surfaces published before rejection in the longer request. Independent forward kinematics at that endpoint agreed to 0.0450 µm in relative position for the four audited bones. The two maximum independently evaluated free-node force residuals were 0.993 mN and 0.578 mN. The minimum regional cell Jacobian was 0.687.

The body was still settling. At the final step the measured support normal force was 1,121.6 N for a model mass of 97.13 kg, and the center of mass was moving upward at 0.0156 m/s. The final geometry had 1,325 transverse triangle pairs across six pelvic muscle–bone banks. Independent closed-solid intersection measured 2.888e-6 mL. At the next candidate the left posterior muscle produced an 11.19 mN free-node residual against a 3.92 mN limit. The qualified duration for zero crossings across the whole body is still open.

Two solver experiments remained outside the selected implementation. Fitting nonnegative pressures at the previously failed geometry left the residual at 14.06 mN on the older contact layout. With stable feature rows, doubling the refresh budget from 16 to 32 repeated an alternating force and contact-release error at step 60. Neither advanced its respective accepted prefix. The iteration trace preserves both sides of that cycle.

The reference construction already contains transverse pairs before any motion. For the same connected boundary in source-world binary64, exact predicates counted 14,523 transverse triangle pairs and 28,217 boundary-only contacts. Closed overlap was approximately 1e-14 mL. After rounding both source surfaces to binary32 in world coordinates, overlap was 7.97e-6 mL and the transverse count was 16,060. Repeating the measurement in each cortical body's local coordinate frame gave 13,444 transverse pairs and 4.25e-6 mL. These are authoring evaluations in specified frames. The separately captured native endpoint uses the executing GPU representation. The counts and enclosed volumes measure different properties. A conforming, conservatively represented unloaded interface is a prerequisite for a credible claim of zero crossings through motion.

The native runtime dependency is the [post-dynamics pose refresh](https://github.com/Numi2/numi-lab/commit/4138685d78e22a6b15826b968dc68cfd5c095790). The additional files contain the binary and source identities, configuration, measured failures, traces and recovery information. The physiology sample and bed reaction come from the coupled resting scene. Sustained breathing, pumping, gas regulation and the throughput target still need qualification through longer motion.
