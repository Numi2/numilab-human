# Registered skin and fat in bed-normal compression

Use the published full-body gravity moment and the registered Float32 outer skin, inner skin and deep fat surfaces. This is an offline test of a proposed equilibrium initializer. The two native constitutive energies are evaluated under either zero transverse strain or zero transverse traction. Skin and fat carry the same nominal pressure in each column. A shared backing translates and tilts until the summed vertical force and both horizontal moments close. No predictor state was supplied to the native simulation.

The densest rule queried 111,766 complete material columns. At first loss of the sampled material determinant limit, the load was approximately 24.82 N with constrained transverse strain or 87.98 N with free transverse traction, against a body weight of 952.86 N. Every engaged ray in these two last valid states had both material exits. At the next coarser rule the limits were 27.71 N and 109.46 N. The variation is material to interpreting the threshold. These numbers characterize the proposed common backing, the chosen ray direction and the sampled flat-bed geometry. A full three-dimensional body can recruit deeper tissues and additional contact areas.

The tables include force and moment residuals, material virtual-work checks and all four spatial sampling levels. Extrapolated loads are marked and their stresses and determinants are suppressed. A ray through a muscle surface measures anatomical occupancy. It cannot establish traction, contact pressure or attachment.

Reproduce the calculations from the retained native package on SSH Numi. Add the adjacent published registered_digit_material tools to PYTHONPATH so the geometry reader uses the same payload and pose convention. Run prepare_bed_columns.py with the parent native-material-ligament-044 directory, a 0.055 m height limit, an output NPZ and one of the subdivisions 1, 2, 4, 8. Run local_pressure_equilibrium.py with that NPZ, the native Matter materials directory, full-mass.json and an output JSON. The NPY array contents are deterministic. ZIP packaging of NPZ files may include timestamps on different library versions. Validate the source hashes and the numerical entries.

The geometric trace uses the original entrance pack, all admitted initial donor frames and the NHTISSUE bank identities. At subdivision 2, the eligible rays weighted at the admissible load limits contained an average 21.9 mm of muscle or tendon before the first bone. An average 53–55 mm fell outside those particular source surfaces. Other tissues and transverse load paths are outside this measurement. The largest weighted muscle contribution came from gluteus maximus. Per-ray results, excluded rays and the complete named-bank ranking are retained.
