## What changed

Compressive contact force is fitted on the current finite supports using the transpose of the same nodal interpolation that defines displacement. A safeguarded correction applies the existing regional elasticity factor to the remaining free force defect. The resulting motion is projected against unilateral constraints on the observed predictor before its contacts, constitutive forces and face intersections are evaluated again. Close-contact multipliers stay associated with their material sites. The controller, pressure law and acceptance thresholds are unchanged. Only passing candidate states publish.

At 20 ms, fitting the tractions alone reduced the left force residual from 296 to 65 µN. Moving the tissue to resolve the remaining force without a unilateral projection left 363 transverse pairs. Projecting that motion kept 16 successive reported endpoints clear. Replacing the working basis unconditionally regressed to nine steps. Limiting replacement to saturated, unresolved trials recovered the next endpoint. The receipts include unsuccessful alternatives and both supported and separated traction fixtures.
