# Native bed-column quadrature on Apple M6

The supported native Human groups its anatomical bed contacts into 32 regions. Each source sample contributes projected area and its registered skin and fat thickness. Within each sample the two layers carry equal pressure in series. Samples within one region share normal approach and carry force in parallel. The previous area-weighted thickness average mixed these two operations.

The corrected region stiffness is the sum of each area divided by its local compliance per unit area. Each reported layer compliance is its share of elastic energy divided by half the squared region force. A common viscous retardation of 12 ms preserves that reduction. Regional geometry still reports area-mean stock thickness. The existing Metal contact Jacobian transfers the force into the skeleton and the existing articulated model owns tissue mass.

An independent quadrature used all 6,248 contributing samples. Native Float32 regional force and separate layer energy agreed with explicit column evaluation to relative errors of 4.8e-8 and 5.4e-8 respectively. The largest stiffness correction was 2.21-fold. Source material moduli remain reference parameters. Peak inferred fat compression in a sampled column was 64 percent, which warrants finite-strain material validation. The 20 mm projected-area selection and single normal displacement per region remain approximations.

Cold launch of the corrected model accepted 8,000 consecutive 2 ms steps in 163.58 wall seconds. The final two seconds averaged 952.73 N of bed support. The reference body weighs approximately 952.9 N. Source mass was 97.13195 kg, reported mass residual and root assistance were zero. The trace counted three breaths and 19 complete filling/ejection cycles. Variable pleural pressure ranged from -770.01 to -470.34 Pa. Launch-to-exit throughput was 0.09781x. The accepted motion window after the first observer callback ran at 0.10495x. The renderer was enabled with 250 presentation callbacks. No continuous video was recorded. The separate full-volume scene still needs supported equilibrium and crossing-free motion.
