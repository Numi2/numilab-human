At the accepted dense endpoint, the 6,568 pairs involve 4,125 unique tissue-face/cortical-bank combinations. Of these, 3,178 already had a recorded triangle-contact force at one or more corners on the same rigid body. In 241, all corners were outside the bone and none recorded a contact on that body. ## Engineering outlook

The reference adult combines anatomical sources. The 32-section control demonstrates a viable cardiac and respiratory integration path. Its measured throughput leaves about 4% margin above 0.1×, with limited presentation frequency. The dense run measures the cost of opening the fully loaded scene over two steps. Extrapolating that rate to a settled body would give a misleading delivery forecast.

The strongest reason to pursue a regional mechanical model is the measured load path. At the retained dense endpoint only 24.1 cm² of the exterior lies within 2 mm of the bed, and the measured skin–bone interface supplies about 1.44 N against an estimated 811.9 N needed by the skeleton if its other forces remain fixed. Local mesh quality improvements and GPU tuning alone cannot establish support. Success in the next session means one posterior region in the coupled scene demonstrably transmits bed load through tissue to bone, with balanced reactions and preserved mass, force, strain and intersection comparisons. If matching that response requires extra springs or loses load at a layer interface, keep the dense benchmark and revise the regional representation before extending it.
