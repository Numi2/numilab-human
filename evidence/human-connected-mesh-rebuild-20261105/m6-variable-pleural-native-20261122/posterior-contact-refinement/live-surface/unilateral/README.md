# Bed support in the coupled momentum solve

The registered skin now carries a unilateral normal impulse and Coulomb friction against the fixed bed. The impulse is solved from the assembled nodal momentum and current clearance. Its derivative includes the coupled FEM operator. Globalization makes the search direction feasible at the support before testing the trial. The fixed bed receives the opposite reaction. The native fresh residual, clearance, material and accepted-step checks still decide whether time advances.

On Apple M6, 8,192 projection and derivative cases passed, covering open, sticking and sliding contact. The maximum finite-difference error per unit mass was 4.68e-7. For the same balanced starting geometry the previous bed implementation accepted zero steps. A coupled impulse alone still failed the first step. With feasible search directions, the native integrated scene accepted two steps of 15.625 microseconds. Step three failed with a fresh nonvascular residual of 0.035747 against 0.0000125. The geometry and collision limits on that trial came from soft tissue. Its bed constraint did not further reduce the permitted step.
