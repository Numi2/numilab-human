# Loaded interface experiment

The two-step reference release has 2,330.616 N of bed force, 952.864 N of weight, and 17.857 N of upward FEM load on an 811.448 N skeleton. The prediction is that relaxing the bonded continuum before release will reduce both the excess bed reaction and the skeletal support deficit. Compare at the same physical step and identical gravity, native control, constitutive, contact and solver settings. A lower starting elastic potential alone cannot qualify this change.

Compare the third-step outcome separately. Keep both loaded and original volume denominators in the independent endpoint check. Reject promotion if the duration regresses, physical source accounting changes, a previously clear skin/fat layer or self-intersection appears, the external skin/tissue crossing count rises at matched time, or the two force discrepancies fail to improve together. A continued moving release still requires full supported equilibrium and the native qualification runs.
