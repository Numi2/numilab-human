# Cardiac wall source decision — 2026-09-12

Use **Rodero et al., CT cohort case 18, Zenodo 4590294 version 1.0.0** as the permanent anatomical source. It is actual CT-derived four-chamber wall geometry, supplies ventricular fibres/sheets, has explicit source mechanics and loading in the accompanying paper, and is distributable under CC BY 4.0. The immediate defensible deliverable is deterministic anatomy/material-source ingestion and strict cavity admission. An accepted native cardiac cycle requires additional constitutive, boundary-condition, unloaded-reference and numerical evidence; the source does not already establish that result.

Authoritative sources:

- [Pinned dataset record](https://zenodo.org/records/4590294), [record API](https://zenodo.org/api/records/4590294), DOI 10.5281/zenodo.4590294, published 2021-03-15. Full creator attribution and CC BY 4.0 URL are in the config.
- [Rodero et al. 2021 article](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008851), *Linking statistical shape models and simulated function in the healthy adult human heart*.
- [S1 geometry/fibre supplement](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article/file?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008851.s001&type=supplementary), SHA256 `08777693dcfd5cfaac676c3e015761d62b68706af21577e678f89808a0c979ba`.
- [S4 mechanics/loading supplement](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article/file?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008851.s004&type=supplementary), SHA256 `5699feb01b7f91f93fd75e26a9a5043ca6bfb5da1803f010c45c71270992ebc6`.

## Verified bytes and concrete format

`18.tar.gz` is 53,661,258 bytes, upstream MD5 `004856c5ebb3f150a87147370b348d6c`, independently acquired SHA256 `b50919a711dc3914cc0a4dd9cabc19679a9f36be9ac6eb26a23ca6799f501720`. Its sole member is `18.vtk`, 154,064,076 bytes, independently verified SHA256 `f0d4f3fc21ea229fa1b334888d374ee606a2915b414fff047764aad44f1c0a41`.

The actual header is VTK legacy 3.0 ASCII `UNSTRUCTURED_GRID`: 300,965 points and 1,470,083 four-node tetrahedra (VTK type 10). Original coordinates are **mm**, to be converted exactly once by 0.001 to metres. Cell fields are `ID`, `fibres`, and `sheets`; point fields are `RHO.dat`, `PHI.dat`, `Z.dat`, `V.dat`. Full headers are retained in `case18-vtk-headers.json`. This is a substantial mesh: no claim that the complete native two-environment runtime already fits or meets useful speed.

The archive contains no separate endocardial surfaces, cap facets, unloaded geometry, original CARP input deck, or time-resolved solution. S1 section S1.2 explicitly obtains endocardia by extracting volume surfaces, finding connected components, and merging their valve/vein planes. Preserve source node identities and generate the oriented cavity boundary from actual tetrahedral incidence. Check closedness, orientation, nonmanifold edges, positive volume, self-intersection and disjoint solid/lumen ownership. Do not replace the surface with an unrelated STL.

The full 24-label map is in `config/cardiac-wall-rodero18.v1.json`. Labels 1–4 are chamber myocardium; 5–6 aortic/pulmonary artery wall; 7–10 artificial mitral/tricuspid/aortic/pulmonary valve layers; 11–17 artificial appendage/vein closure structures; 18–24 truncated appendage/vein borders. Label 11 closes a removed left atrial appendage and must not become an invented vascular connection. The map's port directions are explicitly importer interpretations of anatomy, not supplied flow boundary conditions. Two faces of a valve layer can belong to different chamber boundaries; artificial cap/interface semantics and equal/opposite mechanical ownership must remain explicit.

## Exact mechanics versus missing qualification

S4 uses isochoric Guccione ventricular energy, with `a=1.7 kPa`, `bf=8`, `bfs=4`, `bt=3`, and `kappa=1000 kPa`. Both the Guccione strain and nonventricular neo-Hookean invariant use `Cbar=J^(-2/3) F^T F`; this was visually checked in the source PDF. Nonventricular shear parameters are 7.45 kPa for atria/veins, 26.66 kPa for aorta, 3.7 kPa for pulmonary artery, and 1000 kPa for artificial valve layers. The volumetric penalty is logarithmic for Guccione but quadratic in `J-1` for neo-Hookean. Do not substitute the older Strocchi 2020 HF material table.

S4 gives initial LV/RV pressures 1.6/0.8 kPa, aortic/PA pressures 77/17.4 mmHg, constant atrial-pressure preload, and two three-element Windkessel afterloads. All original resistance/capacitance values and active-tension parameters are transcribed into the config. In particular, source aortic and pulmonary forward valve resistances are **zero**, and large finite reverse resistances approximate blocked flow. Current strictly positive resistance admission does not express this source unchanged; an arbitrary epsilon is not a reproduction.

Ventricular active stress is a phenomenological, hand-tuned Tanh model with 120 kPa peak, 20 ms delay, 50 ms contraction/relaxation and 550 ms duration. Nonventricular tissues are passive. Fibre angles are rule-based (+80° endocardium, −60° epicardium), not measured individual microstructure. S1 assigns literature thicknesses to atria, RV, aorta and PA, removes the left atrial appendage in every case, and creates approximately 2 mm valve closure layers. Blood pools, papillary muscles and trabeculae are excluded from wall tetrahedra.

Missing source inputs remain explicit: the case-specific unloaded reference is absent; retained main article/S4 do not specify density/inertial mass or numerical Robin spring stiffness; main text names superior pulmonary vein anchors while S4 names right pulmonary vein anchors. These are blockers for an exact complete source-model reproduction, not permission to invent defaults. A fixed-wall or isotropic material replacement would only test a separately declared derived model.

All 20 source subjects share parameters, boundary conditions and initial conditions to isolate anatomy. Clinical CT was obtained after acute chest-pain presentations with no cardiac conditions found on follow-up; “healthy” therefore means asymptomatic on that evidence. Source pressure-volume outputs are simulations, not measured subject calibration. Cases 09 and 10 failed to converge; case 18 has a retained output row (LV EDV 92.87 mL, ESV 38.778 mL; RV EDV 119.108 mL, ESV 65.938 mL). Connecting these mechanics to CVSim21 creates a new hybrid; those source outputs do not validate it. No blood mass may be added/subtracted from existing tissue mechanics without a wet-mass attribution owner.

## Alternative sources and exclusions

The strongest compact *restricted research* alternative is [KIT Gerach/Loewe v1.1](https://zenodo.org/records/10526554): 32,505 points, 128,976 tets, four matching cavity STLs, actual MRI anatomy, fibre/sheet/normal fields, cine MRI volume/thickening/AVPD, and detailed [CardioMechanics scenario files](https://github.com/KIT-IBT/CardioMechanics/blob/04a30413213ac183ffa315cb07c015f4c082af7c/examples/JPhys/settings/scenario_I.xml). Dataset license is **CC BY-NC 4.0**, solver code GPL-3.0. Keep it outside unrestricted production anatomy. Mechanical mesh M.vtu is only 8.72 MB; SHA256 `b9b8f4777656fbf1a50d33d553c850f0262ec91614aa6f4beb774711f218aa29`. The actual M.vtu lacks the advertised `Label` array and its sheet vectors are not all unit length; the upstream scenario explicitly enforces orthonormal bases. Its current code pin is `04a30413213ac183ffa315cb07c015f4c082af7c`. Exact downloads and config pins are retained separately here. Do not fetch its 640 MB EP mesh for mechanical admission.

[SimVascular Land-2015 LV](https://github.com/SimVascular/svMultiPhysics/tree/00f5c9204bb5a9991e4c8d18eddd3b1492f3b59a/tests/cases/ustruct/LV_Guccione_active) and [cardiac_benchmark](https://github.com/finsberg/cardiac_benchmark/tree/c7d2d1ba6d680627dc465598dbf6b0363a75d604) provide useful law/solver comparisons but explicitly use idealized ellipsoidal geometry. They do not satisfy this anatomical task. The newer benchmark bulk Zenodo archive is 23.18 GB and was not downloaded. Older Strocchi 2020 HF archives are 0.70–1.28 GB per subject and were also not downloaded.

Local source review found circulation models and BodyParts3D cardiac surface work, but no existing matched anatomical wall/fibre/material dataset. All research downloads stayed in this owned scratch directory. No native builds, GPU runs, source equation stepping or production-code changes were performed. The only new tracked artifact is the explicit source config requested by the parent.
